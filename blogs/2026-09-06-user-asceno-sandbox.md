His films include the short documentary ''[[Arsy-Versy]]'' (2009), the feature documentaries ''Comeback'' (2014), ''Cooltúra'' (2016), ''Richard Müller: This Is Not Me'' (2016) and ''At Full Throttle'' (2021).
