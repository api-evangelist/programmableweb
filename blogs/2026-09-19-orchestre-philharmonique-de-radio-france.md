History: format ← Previous revision Revision as of 20:44, 19 September 2026 Line 19: Line 19: ''[[Radiodiffusion Française]]'' established the orchestra in Paris in June 1937 under the name of the ''Orchestre Radio-Symphonique'', under the auspices of ''Les Postes, Télégraphes et Téléphones'' (PTT) and its minister, Robert Jardillier. The orchestra was initially under the direction of [[Rhené-Baton]], who guided the orchestra until his death in 1940. Eugène Bigot subsequently directed the orchestra musicians through the 1944 Liberation.
