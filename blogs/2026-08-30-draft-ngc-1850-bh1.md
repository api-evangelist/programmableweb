← Created page with 'NGC 1850 BH1 is a binary star system located in the cluster NGC 1850 in the Large Magellanic Cloud. NGC 1850 BH1 (Black Hole 1) was discovered by a team led by Sara Saracino in 2022; the team was systematically searching for black holes in the LMC. They discovered a binary system containing an approximately 5-solar-mass main-sequence turnoff star which was beginning to fill its Roche lobe and an unseen binary companion.
