cleanup ← Previous revision Revision as of 16:09, 1 August 2026 Line 16: Line 16: }} }} '''Allen's swamp monkey''' ('''''Allenopithecus nigroviridis''''') is a species of [[Old World monkey]] endemic to [[swamp]]s and [[riparian forest]]s in the central [[Congo Basin]]. As a medium-sized [[primate]], adult individuals can have head-body lengths of up to {{cvt|18|inches|cm}}, with tails being slightly longer. It is identifiable by its coarse coat of green, brown, gray, and white fur.
